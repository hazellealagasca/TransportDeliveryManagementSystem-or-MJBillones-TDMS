Food quality and safety are important concerns in the production and distribution of perishable goods. Bakeries and bakeshops, especially large and well-known brands, rely heavily on an efficient and reliable cold chain system to preserve the freshness, taste, and overall quality of the products. In the Philippines, companies such as Goldilocks and Red Ribbon are among the leading bakeshop chains that distribute cakes, pastries, and other perishable products to numerous branches. To ensure that these products reach destinations in optimal conditions, the role of refrigerated or cold transport services has become increasingly important. 
MJ Billones Transport Service is a trucking company owned by Marie Ann J. Billones that provides transport services to the franchises of Goldilocks and Red Ribbon that includes Millenium FoodBite Corporation, BCS group of company,Bestbake Incorporation and Richbake Incorporation,which began its operations on September 23, 2017, located at Jasmine Street Barangay San Francisco, Tarlac, City.
Figure 1. MJ Billones Transport Service
The company is responsible for delivering frozen and chilled goods from the commissaries to different stores. These deliveries must be carried out within a specific time frame and under required temperature conditions to sustain product quality. The company has a total of 13 drivers, 23 aides, 3 mechanics, 1 fabricator   and 1 office assistant. In normal days, the company has only 8 operating trucks per day, while in peak season, the company has 18 operating trucks per day. The MJ Billones Transport Services delivery coverage includes Pampanga, Nueva Ecija, Aurora, Isabela, Kalinga, La union, Ilocos Sur, Ilocos Norte, Cordillera Administrative Region.

The company's rate matrix, as shown in Figure 4, is utilized to calculate standardized delivery costs. Figure 5. Loading Schedule
As shown in Figure 5, the loading schedule is sent by the commissary to M.J. Billones Transport Service to determine the required truck capacity for dispatch. Upon arrival at the commissary, Quality Assurance (QA) personnel inspect the truck's temperature to ensure it meets the requirements for loading perishable goods, followed by an initial count of the total crates. Once the temperature is verified, the driver and the delivery aide load the products and perform a secondary count to confirm accuracy. Prior to departure, QA personnel provide the crew with the delivery receipt and the franchise trip report.

Purpose and Description
The primary purpose of this study is to design and develop a Transport Delivery Management System for M.J. Billones Transport Service that will support the handling of delivering chilled and dry goods with its clients. Given the increasing demand for efficient logistics in the food industry and the need for more systematic operations at M.J. Billones Transport, there is a clear opportunity to improve the company’s processes through the development of a management system.
By providing a computerized solution for loading schedule, employee management, delivery scheduling, and trip delivery of information, the study aims to improve the existing manual processes with an organized and efficient system. The project also seeks to provide a basis for evaluating how the proposed system can help improve the accuracy of records, timeliness of deliveries, and overall transactions of the company.
 The proposed project is intended for internal use by M.J. Billones Transport. It focuses on the main functions needed to manage daily transport operations, including user login and security, crew management, delivery scheduling, loading schedule, and basic report generation that includes total trips per day. Through a database, authorized users will be able to input, update, and retrieve essential data related to deliveries with the franchises of Goldilocks and Red Ribbon.
The system will provide interfaces for creating and viewing delivery schedules, assigning trucks, aides and drivers to specific trips, and marking deliveries as completed, pending, or cancelled. It will generate summary reports on daily deliveries, deliveries per client, and trips, which can assist management in monitoring business transactions. The project will be developed using appropriate programming tools and will be deployed on desktop or laptop computers within the company premises.
Objectives 
General Objectives 
To design, develop, and evaluate the Transport Delivery Management System for M.J. Billones. The system will gather and organize loading schedules, trip details, record billing, and payment.  Its goal is to improve record-keeping, improve documentation, and make it easier to gather and organize records.
Specific Objectives 
To design and develop a module that manages system configuration and user accounts.
This module will provide centralized digital storage and organization of important operational records, supporting updating, searching, filtering, and archiving functions to ensure business documents remain accurate, complete, and easily accessible.
By giving the owner and staff individual login accounts, the module ensures that users only have access to the specific tools and records required for their job roles. This prevents unauthorized changes to delivery logs, protects confidential company information, and creates a safe, organized workspace where every user's actions can be properly tracked.
To design and develop a module for loading management.
This module will record loading schedules and determine the appropriate truck capacity based on the number and size of crates. It will support efficient truck scheduling, manual tagging of peak and non-peak schedules, and coordination to ensure on-time deliveries.
To design and develop a module that will handle truck management.
This module will monitor truck availability, status, and record which truck is assigned to each delivery. It will help the system plan and process deliveries more efficiently.
To design and develop a module for crew management.
This module will store driver and aide names and contact information. It will also check driver and aide availability and assist in assigning driver and aides to deliveries. This will reduce the time scheduling and make driver and aide assignments more efficient. By tracking crew availability and matching names directly to trips, the system helps employees quickly see who is free or already on the road. This reduces the time spent checking staff schedules and prevents the mistake of double-booking the same driver or aide. It serves as an efficient tool that coordinates assignments smoothly, ensuring that every delivery truck has a designated crew assigned to it without confusion.
To design and develop a module that will process trip documentation and reports.
This module will allow authorized personnel to record key details from plant-issued trip documents including scheduled trips reports, trucks, drivers and aides information, and details of goods that are being transported. It will also provide options to attach digital copies of the original documents for easy reference.
To design and develop a module that will handle billing.
This module will record trip details, generate statements of account, and produce invoices to support accurate billing and easier payment tracking.
Scope and Limitations
The proposed Transport Delivery Management System is designed to improve the daily operational processes of M.J. Billones Transport Service by computerizing account management, crew and truck management, loading schedules, dispatching, trip documentation, billing. While the system provides a more efficient and organized method of handling transport delivery records, it is limited to basic office-based operations and does not include advanced features such as full payroll processing, automated scheduling, GPS tracking, or real-time temperature monitoring
Scope
Account Management Module
This module provides secure access to the system through username and password authentication. It ensures that only authorized users can access the system based on their assigned roles.
Functions included:
• Creation of user accounts
• Update of username and password
• Archiving or deactivation of inactive accounts
• Role-based user access for owner and staff
This module helps protect sensitive business data and ensures confidentiality and controlled access to records.
Dashboard Module
This module serves as the main overview page of the system. It displays summarized and updated information that helps users monitor daily operations.
Functions included:
• Display of total deliveries
• Display of scheduled deliveries for the day
• Display of pending trips
• Display of available trucks
This module gives the owner and staff a quick summary of the company’s delivery status and transaction updates.
Crew Management Module
This module stores and manages crew records, particularly the drivers, aides, and on-call crew assigned to deliveries.
Functions included:
• Recording of crew information such as name, role, and contact number
• Monitoring of driver and aide availability
• Assignment of drivers and aides to delivery schedules
• Viewing of driver and aide trip schedules
This module helps reduce scheduling time and improves crew assignment efficiency.
Truck Management Module
This module manages all truck-related records used in transport operations.
Functions included:
• Recording of truck details such as truck type and plate number.
• Monitoring of truck availability and status
• Tagging trucks as available, in use, inactive, or under maintenance
• Tracking which truck is assigned to each delivery
This module supports more efficient delivery planning and truck utilization.
Loading Management Module
This module is responsible for recording and organizing loading schedules received from the commissary or client.
Functions included:
• Encoding of load schedules
• Recording of destination, client, schedule date, and total crates
• Determination of truck capacity based on the number and size of crates
• Coordination of truck scheduling for delivery preparation
• Manual tagging of peak and non-peak schedules
This module improves schedule coordination and helps ensure that deliveries are prepared properly and completed on time.
Delivery and Dispatch Management Module
This module handles the scheduling and dispatching of deliveries.
Functions included:
• Creation and assignment of delivery schedules
• Assignment of truck, driver, and aide for each trip
• Viewing and updating of daily and weekly schedules
• Delivery tagging as completed, pending, delayed, or cancelled
• Recording of departure details and dispatch status
This module allows staff to monitor delivery movement and update the status of each trip more efficiently.
Trip Report Module
This module records key details from plant-issued trip documents and delivery completion reports.
Functions included:
• Recording of trip report number
• Recording of assigned truck, driver, and aides
• Encoding of arrival and departure time
• Recording of delivered and returned crates
• Recording of store manager confirmation and remarks
This module provides digital trip documentation for accountability and future references.
Incident Report Module
This module records delivery-related incidents, irregularities, and damaged goods encountered during transport operations.
Functions included:
• Encoding of damage report details
• Recording of damaged quantity and estimated cost
• Recording of cause of damage
• Marking whether the damage is chargeable to the crew
• Recording of incidents, delays, and irregularities for accountability
This module improves monitoring of delivery issues and supports documentation for loss and incident reporting.
Billing Module
 This module manages the billing process for completed trips.
Functions included:
• Recording of trip details for billing
• Generation of statement of account
• Generation of service invoice
This module supports accurate trip billing and makes payment tracking easier.
Collection Module
This module records payments received from clients for completed transport services.
Functions included:
• Recording of payment amount
• Recording of payment date and method
• Updating billing status as unpaid, paid, or overdue
This module helps track receivables and organize payment transactions properly.
System Configuration Module
This module provides centralized digital storage and organization of important operational records.
Functions included:
• Updating and maintaining records
• Searching and filtering records
• Retrieving important documents and transaction history
• Archiving inactive records
• Maintaining accurate and complete business data
This module ensures that records remain organized, accessible, and easy to manage.
Reports and Data Viewing Module
This module allows users to generate and view summaries of transport operations and transactions.
Functions included:
• Daily delivery reports
• Truck overview reports
• Driver and aide overview reports
• Billing and collection reports
• Remarks and incident logs
• Search and filter functions by date, client, driver, destination, or status
This module helps management review operational performance and monitor records more effectively.
Data Backup and Restore Module
This module provides a basic backup and restore function to prevent data loss and maintain continuity of records.
Functions included:
• Creating backup copies of the database
• Restoring previously saved records in case of system failure
This module helps protect important transport and billing records from accidental loss.
Limitations
No Payroll Processing
The system only records the daily wage per trip and food allowance of drivers and aides for documentation purposes. It does not include complete payroll computation, salary deductions, benefits, tax, or other payroll processes.
No Automated Scheduling
The system does not automatically generate or suggest delivery schedules based on truck availability, crew workload, or route optimization. All schedules and assignments are entered manually by authorized staff.
No GPS or Real-Time Vehicle Tracking
The system does not include GPS integration or live vehicle location tracking. Delivery status updates are manually entered by staff.
The system will exclude asset maintenance 
The system will not include the asset maintenance of the trucks.
No automated Backup and Restore 
The backup and restore function will be done manually and are limited to local database storage

As a result, both foreign and local sources strongly emphasize the importance of improving logistics systems through better organization and technological support.
A common theme across both literature and studies is the necessity of adopting information technology to enhance logistics operations. Foreign research often focuses on advanced solutions such as automation, Internet of Things (IoT) integration, real-time monitoring, and data-driven decision-making, as discussed in studies by Macharis and Melo (2024), the World Bank (2018), and Wang (2021). In contrast, local studies—including those involving FAST Logistics (2023), delivery services in Lipa City (2022), and Mendoza and De Guzman (2023)—highlight the practical realities of logistics businesses, where many still rely on manual systems that result in inefficiencies and miscommunication. This comparison reveals a gap between highly sophisticated technological solutions and the actual needs of small- to medium-sized transport enterprises. The proposed Cold Transport Delivery Management System for M.J. Billones Transport addresses this gap by offering a simplified yet effective platform for scheduling, monitoring, and reporting.
In conclusion, the reviewed literature and studies support the development of a transport management system as a practical solution to common logistics challenges. By replacing manual processes with a centralized digital system, the proposed project improves coordination, enhances data organization, and supports better decision-making. Despite differences in scale and technological complexity, both foreign and local sources agree that structured data management and systematization are essential for achieving efficient, reliable, and well-coordinated logistics operations. Therefore, the proposed system is aligned with existing research and contributes to improving operational efficiency in the trucking and delivery industry.

Design of Software, System, Product, and/or Processes
The developers will apply the Agile Scrum development methodology to support the design and development of the Transport Delivery Management System for MJ Billones Transport Service. Agile Scrum is a widely adopted framework for managing and delivering work through incremental cycles. In Scrum, the developers plan, implement, and evaluate progress in time-boxed iterations called sprints, typically lasting between one and four weeks. Throughout the sprint process, developers organize and manage the required work in a product backlog, where tasks are prioritized and refined based on stakeholder needs and evolving requirements. At the end of each sprint, developers conduct a sprint review to present outcomes and collect feedback, followed by a sprint retrospective to assess performance and identify actionable improvements for the next sprint.
Figure 12. Agile Scrum Methodology
Sprint Planning 
Sprint Planning is the meeting that initiates the cycle of each development cycle. During this phase, the developers and the client collaborate to establish a specific sprint goal. They evaluate the project backlog to select high-priority tasks and determine the necessary steps to achieve them. This stage ensures that the team’s efforts are strategically aligned with the overall project objectives.
Daily Scrum 
The Daily Scrum is a brief, time-boxed synchronization meeting held every 24 hours. The developers use this opportunity to share progress updates, outline daily objectives, and identify any technical obstacles. This practice promotes transparency and allows for immediate adjustments, ensuring that the project maintains its momentum.
Development 
The Development phase, the developers focus on the technical execution of the tasks assigned in the sprint backlog. This process involves design, implementation, and rigorous testing to ensure every feature meets quality standards. Work is performed in an iterative manner, allowing for constant refinement and the delivery of a functional product to increase by the end of the cycle.
Sprint Review
The Sprint Review is a formal session held at the conclusion of a sprint to evaluate the work. During this meeting, the developers present the finished results to the client. The developers demonstrate a live version of the new features to show progress. The client provides feedback and opinions on the quality of the work. This discussion identifies specific items that need improvement or adjustment. This process ensures that the project remains aligned with the goals of the business and the needs of the client.
Sprint Retrospective 
The final step is the Sprint Retrospective, which focuses on the internal process. The Developers, Scrum Master, and Product Owner look back on the completed sprint together. They discuss what was successful, identify any mistakes made, and agree on specific changes to improve efficiency and teamwork in the next cycle.
